During my last spring semester, I created traditional art in a design and drawing class I took at San Jacinto College.

Project 1: Cardboard boat

This project is artwork from my design class, as it was my final project for the class. The assignment was to replicate and modify an artist's artwork through cardboard and paint. My piece was based on an abstract artist named Paul Klee. I chose one of his art pieces called "Sailing ships". Klee had many colors involved in his painting of a boat and shapes in multiple colors constructing a boat. What I modified in my art piece was to show the boat more and make the sky through square-like shapes to indicate the sky more in his painting.

Project 2: Circles for days

This project is an art piece from my design project back in February of this year. The mediums I used were ink pens and a black alcohol marker, while the surface I used was Bristol paper. This assignment's point was to make patterns by using a ruler or a compass while making a pattern using white and black colors. The process I used in the project was to use circle objects to trace my smaller circles and use a compass to make a series of circles. Then I colored the circles black, not on all of them, but I wanted to show movement throughout the other circles in my project.

Project 3: Inside of a circus

This project I created was my final drawing project from my drawing class. This piece took time to understand where my subjects go and what occurs in the project, as my characters are circus performers getting prepared for their circus acts. The mediums I used for this one were alcohol markers, ink pens, and white pens to make this piece.
